# Calibration ablation for the sleep model (REQ-FEAT-10)

Float dense net from `scripts/train_sleep_model.py`, 20 subjects, the same 5-fold leave-subjects-out
split. Only the per-subject standardisation statistics change. Generated by `scripts/calibration_ablation.py`.

| Standardisation | Accuracy | Macro F1 | Recall W | Recall N1 | Recall N2 | Recall N3 | Recall REM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| trained on whole night, tested on whole night | 0.796 | 0.740 | 0.83 | 0.52 | 0.81 | 0.83 | 0.80 |
| trained on whole night, tested on first 60 s | 0.569 | 0.450 | 0.20 | 0.30 | 0.83 | 0.53 | 0.38 |
| trained on whole night, tested on first 5 min | 0.577 | 0.462 | 0.20 | 0.27 | 0.83 | 0.52 | 0.46 |
| trained on whole night, tested on first 10 min | 0.581 | 0.445 | 0.16 | 0.16 | 0.85 | 0.59 | 0.45 |
| trained on whole night, tested on running | 0.729 | 0.654 | 0.53 | 0.38 | 0.82 | 0.73 | 0.79 |
| trained+tested on first 5 min | 0.679 | 0.602 | 0.77 | 0.27 | 0.72 | 0.64 | 0.66 |
| trained+tested on running | 0.770 | 0.710 | 0.75 | 0.47 | 0.79 | 0.78 | 0.83 |

Baseline random forest (raw features, no standardisation): accuracy 0.774, macro F1 0.680.

**Reading:** the model in `reports/sleep_model.md` relies on whole-night statistics. With statistics
from a short calibration at the start of the recording (wake), it falls well below the random forest.
A causal running mean/SD (device: Welford update over 30 values per epoch, 10-epoch warm-up),
used for training as well as testing, keeps most of the gain and still beats the random forest.
